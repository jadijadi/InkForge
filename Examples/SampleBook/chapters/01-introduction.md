# Introduction

Every generation believes it lives at the hinge of history. Ours might actually be right.

In the span of a single lifetime, the way humans store, share and shape ideas has changed
more than in the previous five centuries combined. This book is about that change — not the
gadgets, but the *habits of mind* they quietly rewired.

## What this book is not

- It is not a history of computing.
- It is not a manifesto, for or against technology.
- It is **not** a prediction of the future.

> "The medium is the message." — Marshall McLuhan

We will look at three shifts:

1. The network, which made distance irrelevant.
2. The feed, which made attention the scarce resource.
3. The agent, which made *doing* as cheap as *asking*.
