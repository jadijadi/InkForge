# Artificial Intelligence

For most of computing's history, the machine did exactly what it was told — nothing more,
nothing less. Large language models broke that contract.

## From instructions to intentions

You no longer specify *how*. You describe *what*, and negotiate the rest.

Consider a writer working with an agent:

- The writer opens a chapter.
- The agent is asked to "make this accessible to a general audience."
- The file changes. The writer reviews, keeps some edits, rejects others.

That loop — ask, watch, review — is the subject of this chapter.

## What stays human

Taste. Judgment. Responsibility. The agent can draft; only you can decide.
